Here is the **Duval County, Florida foreclosure and county lead source map**.

Important distinction: this is **Duval County, Florida, Jacksonville area**, not Duval County, Texas.

Duval is one of the cleaner counties because the two main auction lanes are actually online:

1. **Judicial foreclosure auctions**: RealForeclose
2. **Tax deed auctions**: RealTaxDeed
3. **Tax certificate sale**: LienHub

---

# 1. Official online foreclosure auction portals

## 1. Duval County Judicial Foreclosure Auction Portal

https://www.duval.realforeclose.com/index.cfm

Official online auction portal for Duval County mortgage foreclosures, HOA
foreclosures, lien foreclosures, and court-ordered Chapter 45 foreclosure sales.
All foreclosure auctions are held online through this portal; sales Monday–Friday,
first sale 11:00 AM. Use for: notice-of-sale properties, final judgment sales,
HOA foreclosure sales, mortgage foreclosure sales, canceled/rescheduled auctions,
auction calendar monitoring.

## 2. Duval Clerk Foreclosure Information Page

https://www.duvalclerk.gov/departments/civil-court-services/foreclosure

Official Clerk explanation page for foreclosure sales — links to RealForeclose,
explains the sale process, directs to CORE for case research. Use for: auction
rules, third-party bidder info, payment rules, certificate of sale info,
assignment of bid info, surplus funds explanation.

# 2. Official tax deed auction portals

## 3. Duval County Tax Deed Auction Portal

https://www.duval.realtaxdeed.com/index.cfm

Official online auction portal for Duval County tax deed sales (Fla. Stat.
197.542). Use for: tax deed auction calendar, upcoming/canceled/closed tax deed
properties, bidder registration, auction deposits.

## 4. Duval Clerk Tax Deed Files Page

https://www.duvalclerk.com/departments/county-services/tax-deed-files

Official Clerk page for tax deed sale rules. Clerk posts the sale date and
property list 30 days before the auction; also advertised in the Financial News
and Daily Record public notices. Use for: tax deed process, posting timeline,
redemption rules, deposit rules, surviving-lien warnings, surplus forms.

## 5. Duval County Tax Deed File Search

https://taxdeed.duvalclerk.com/

Search tax deed files by sale date, certificate number, case number, parcel ID,
tax collector number, applicant, owner, status, lands available, surplus funds.
Statuses to watch: SALE, SOLD, UNSOLD, REDEEMED, REMOVESALE, RESCHED, LANDS
AVAILABLE, BANKRUPTCY, ESCHEATED.

## 6. Duval Tax Certificate Sale Portal

https://lienhub.com/duval

Annual Duval County tax certificate sale (NOT a property auction — sale of
delinquent tax certificates / liens). 2026 sale: May 27, 2026; registration
opens May 8, 2026; online at LienHub. Use for: delinquent tax certificate leads,
tax lien position research, future tax deed application tracking.

## 7. Duval Tax Collector Investor Guide

https://taxcollector.jacksonville.gov/taxes/property-taxes/tax-sale%2C-certificates%2C-and-tax-deeds

Explains tax certificates vs tax deeds; certificates are liens not ownership; a
certificate must generally be held before a tax deed application. Use for:
certificate rules, investor warnings, sale timing, tax deed application path.

# 3. Clerk records, court records, and foreclosure case research

## 8. Duval Clerk CORE Court Records Portal

https://core.duvalclerk.com/

Clerk court case portal. Foreclosure case info viewable by case number,
plaintiff name, or defendant name. Use for: foreclosure cases, final judgments,
motions to cancel sale, orders resetting sale, certificates of sale/title, HOA
foreclosure lawsuits, tax lawsuits, probate cases when available.

## 9. CORE Public Access Entry

https://core.duvalclerk.com/CoreCms.aspx?mode=PublicAccess

Direct public-access loading page for CORE. JavaScript-heavy. Use when you have:
case number, plaintiff/defendant/party name, civil division.

## 10. Duval Clerk Official Records Search

https://or.duvalclerk.com/search/

Official land records portal — Duval official records recorded since 1988. Use
for: deeds, mortgages, assignments, lis pendens, notice-of-sale documents,
judgments, liens, satisfactions, releases, certificates of title, tax deeds,
probate-related recorded documents.

## 11. Official Records Name Search

https://or.duvalclerk.com/search/SearchTypeName

Search by grantor, grantee, borrower, owner, lender, bank, trustee, HOA,
investor entity, or estate name.

## 12. Official Records Document Type Search

https://or.duvalclerk.com/search/SearchTypeDocType

Search by document type — isolate recorded instruments. Distress doc types: Lis
Pendens, Notice, Mortgage, Assignment, Claim of Lien, Judgment, Tax Deed,
Certificate of Title, Satisfaction, Release, Lien.

## 13. Official Records Record Date Search

https://or.duvalclerk.com/search/SearchTypeRecordDate

Daily/weekly pulls of newly recorded documents — fresh distress signals. Pull
yesterday / last 7 days / last 30 days; filter for LP, liens, judgments,
certificates, assignments.

## 14. Official Records Case Number Search

https://or.duvalclerk.com/search/  (choose Case #)

Connect recorded land records to court cases when you have the foreclosure case
number from CORE or public notices.

## 15. Duval Property Fraud Alert

https://www.duvalclerk.com/services/property-fraud-alert

Not a lead list — a monitoring tool. Alerts when a document matching a selected
name is recorded. Use for tracking owners, investor entities, target LLCs, deed
activity, suspicious conveyances.

## 16. Duval Clerk E Notify

https://www.duvalclerk.com/online-option/e-notify

Court date notifications by text/email. Use for monitoring active foreclosure
cases, hearing dates, probate cases, civil cases tied to title issues.

# Best Duval County workflow

## For judicial foreclosure leads
1. Pull active auction calendar: duval.realforeclose.com/index.cfm
2. Search foreclosure notices: legals.jaxdailyrecord.com (Notice of Sale - Foreclosure)
3. Pull case details: core.duvalclerk.com
4. Pull recorded docs: or.duvalclerk.com/search/
5. Verify owner and parcel: paopropertysearch.coj.net/Basic/Search.aspx
6. Check taxes: duval.county-taxes.com/public
7. Check municipal liens: tclieninfo.coj.net
8. Check code/permit distress: jaxepics.coj.net + jacksonville.gov/mccd

## For tax deed leads
1. Tax deed auction portal: duval.realtaxdeed.com/index.cfm
2. Search tax deed files: taxdeed.duvalclerk.com
3. Monitor lands available + surplus funds: taxdeed.duvalclerk.com
4. Research certificates: lienhub.com/duval + Tax Collector investor guide
5. Verify property/owner/values/maps: paopropertysearch.coj.net + maps.coj.net/duvalproperty
6. Check liens and recorded documents: or.duvalclerk.com/search/

## Source of truth order
1. RealForeclose — foreclosure auctions
2. RealTaxDeed — tax deed auctions
3. TaxDeed Duval Clerk file search — tax deed cases, lands available, surplus
4. CORE — court case activity
5. Official Records — deeds, liens, LPs, title documents
6. Property Appraiser — ownership and parcel verification
7. Tax Collector — taxes and certificates
8. Municipal lien and code portals — extra distress
9. Third-party portals — radar and filtering only
